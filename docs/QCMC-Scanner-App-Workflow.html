Physical Count

Blind count. ERP quantity, expected quantity, variance, and above/below indicators are never shown to the scanner operator. Offline scanning is supported.

Scan Reconciliation QRValidate that the Physical Count is open.
→
Scan Storage LocationStart or resume the location.
→
Scan ItemsChoose ADD or DEDUCT and enter barcode count.
→
Save Audit TransactionUse an immutable transaction ID.
→
Complete LocationOr confirm Mark Location Empty.
→
Sync / Next LocationQueue offline or save to ERP, then continue.
When offline

Scans and status changes remain on the device as Pending Sync. Safe retries keep the original submission and transaction IDs.

Before completion

Every in-scope location must be Counted or Empty, and all offline records must be synchronized.

Receive Finished Goods

Online only. ERPNext is the source of truth. The workflow is divided across Pullout, Checker, and Picker devices or roles.

1 · Pullout

Scan Job Card QRValidate live Job Card data in ERPNext.
↓
Scan ItemEnter count, Actual Weight per Item, and Pull Out Slip.
↓
Create Draft Stock EntryMultiple Pullout drafts may belong to one Job Card.
↓
Create Handover BatchMake the batch available for Checker review.

2 · Checker

Open Handover BatchReview each Stock Entry, item, and quantity.
↓
Correct or ConfirmQuantity and Actual Weight remain editable per item.
↓
Scan Checker QRConfirm the assigned active Checker.
↓
Submit PulloutsGenerate the backend-issued Picker Handover QR.

3 · Picker

Scan Handover QRLoad the checked transaction on the Picker device.
↓
Create AllocationSelect transaction type, warehouse, date, and time.
↓
Verify PlacementScan each location and confirm actual quantity.
↓
Reallocate BalanceERPNext suggests another location when quantity remains.
↓
Complete AllocationSubmit allocation and linked records; remove finished active work.
